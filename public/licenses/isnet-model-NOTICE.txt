Changes: the original weights were converted to the ONNX format, and the
convolution weights are stored as 8-bit integers with one scale per output
channel, so the file is small enough for a web browser. They are turned back
into full-precision numbers before use, and all maths runs in full precision. The files are served as four
chunks in /model/ and are reassembled by the page before use.
