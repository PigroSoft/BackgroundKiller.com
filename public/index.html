Questions

Free, with no sign-up and no watermark? Smells like a setup, and in this town you learn to check the exits. So I checked, and here is the case file: your computer does the cutting, so there is no server bill to pass on, which leaves nothing to charge you for. No angle, no catch, no guy waiting in the back room.

Is Background Killer really free?

Yes. There are no credits, accounts or watermarks, and you can cut out as many photos as you like.

Is this a remove.bg alternative?

Yes. remove.bg has announced that its standalone website will close on 1 December 2026. Background Killer does the same basic job, and the free download is full size. Here is how they compare.

Do you upload my photo?

No. The cutout model runs in your browser, on your own computer, and the page never sends your photo anywhere. The model is about 47 MB, so it downloads the first time you visit and is saved for next time. You can check this yourself: here is how.

How big can the download be?

It matches your original photo, pixel for pixel. Nothing is scaled down. Very large photos use a lot of memory, so on an older phone a huge image may fail. If that happens, try a smaller version.

How do I remove the background from a photo?

Click Upload image, or drop a photo on the page, and wait a moment for the cutout. Then press Download full size. For a white, black or colored background, open Clean getaway and pick one first. The full steps are here.

Which files work?

JPG, PNG and WebP. HEIC photos work in browsers that can open them, such as Safari. Downloads are PNG or WebP, and both keep transparency.

What if it misses a spot?

Open Sharpen the claws, choose Erase to brush away leftovers, or Restore to bring back anything it cut by mistake. Undo is right there.

What are the checkerboard squares?

They show transparent areas in the preview. They are not part of the file you download.

Can I do a lot of photos at once?

Yes. Drop in 20 or more at once, or add more while others are still cutting. The newest opens right away, the rest keep going in the background, and the little previews at the bottom jump between them. When they are done, Download all gives you one zip of full-size cutouts. This is handy for product photos.

What if the cutout is wrong?

Open Something fishy? and try another way. There are four: keep proportions, zoom in on the subject, double check, and boost contrast. Each runs the model differently, and you can switch back to the first try at any time. Then tidy anything left with Erase and Restore.

That is Claw. He handles the backgrounds.
