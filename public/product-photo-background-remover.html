Remove backgrounds from product photos

Clean, consistent backgrounds make a shop look tidier, and many marketplaces expect a plain white background on the main photo. Background Killer cuts out a whole batch of product photos and saves them as one zip.

Remove a background

Several photos in five steps

  1. Drop in all your photos at once, or add more at any time. They are cut out one after another.
  2. Click each preview at the bottom to check the result. If one is off, try Cutout not right?, then fix edges with Erase and Restore.
  3. Choose the white swatch for a pure white background. Marketplaces such as Amazon ask for pure white on main images, so check the current rules for the one you sell on.
  4. Keep Trim to the subject switched on and set the same margin for every photo, so the products look consistent.
  5. Choose PNG or WebP and press Download all as .zip.

Take photos that cut out well

  • Shoot on a plain surface in soft light.
  • Keep the whole product in the frame, with a little space around it.
  • Avoid white products on a white surface. The model needs some contrast to find the edge.
  • Shoot at full camera resolution. The download keeps the size you give it, and many marketplaces want at least 1,000 pixels on the long side.

Things to know

  • Shadows disappear with the background. If you want a soft shadow under the product, add one in your editor afterwards.
  • Glass, bottles and shiny edges are hard for any automatic tool. Expect to use the brush on them.
  • Your photos are processed on your own computer and are never uploaded, which is useful for unreleased products.
