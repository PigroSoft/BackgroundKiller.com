BackgroundKiller.com

Privacy

Background Killer removes backgrounds inside your browser. The photos you add are processed on your own device and are not uploaded to us or to anyone else.

What the site stores

The cutout model (about 46 MB) is saved in your browser's cache so it only downloads once. It contains no personal information, and you can clear it from your browser settings at any time. The site does not use accounts, cookies for tracking, or analytics.

What the host sees

The site is served by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare handles your IP address and the pages your browser requests in order to deliver the site and keep it secure. Your photos are not part of those requests.

Ads

The site may show ads in the future. If that happens, the advertising provider may set cookies or collect data under its own policy, we will ask for your consent where the law requires it, and this page will be updated first.
