The cutout model

The model that finds the subject in your photo is ISNet general-use, from the DIS project (Dichotomous Image Segmentation) by Xuebin Qin and colleagues, released under the Apache License 2.0. It is described in “Highly Accurate Dichotomous Image Segmentation” (ECCV 2022). We converted it to the ONNX format and compressed it to 8 bits so that it runs in a browser. It runs entirely on your device.

Edge refinement

Background Killer snaps the model's mask to the edges of your photo with a guided filter (Kaiming He and colleagues, 2010) and removes color spill from the old background with blur fusion (Thomas Germer and colleagues, 2020; Marco Forte and François Pitié, 2021). Both are our own small implementations of published methods, running on your device.
